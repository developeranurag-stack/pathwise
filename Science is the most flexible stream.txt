Science is the **most flexible stream in India**. A science student (PCM, PCB, or PCMB) can enter not only engineering and medicine, but also management, law, design, government, finance, defense, research, entrepreneurship, and many emerging technology careers. ([Appli][1])

Below is one of the most exhaustive career maps available.
