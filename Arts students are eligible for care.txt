Arts students are eligible for careers across government, private, entrepreneurial, creative, technology, and interdisciplinary fields—not just teaching or civil services. Modern careers increasingly value skills in communication, critical thinking, psychology, law, design, research, and creativity, all of which are core strengths of humanities education. ([Careers360][1])
