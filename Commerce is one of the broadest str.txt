Commerce is one of the broadest streams in India. A commerce student can build a career not only in finance and accounting but also in consulting, technology, law, analytics, public policy, entrepreneurship, media, logistics, healthcare, and the government sector. The traditional perception that commerce students are limited to CA, banking, or B.Com is no longer accurate. ([upGrad][1])

Below is a comprehensive master list of career options available to commerce students in India.
